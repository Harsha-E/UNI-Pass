How UNI-PASS Works

Three simple steps to secure authorization.

1. Request

Student submits a digital request. Logic checks if it's medical, personal, or OD.

2. Approve

Class Teacher reviews it. If > 2 days, it auto-escalates to the HOD.

3. Verify

A cryptographically signed PDF is generated. Security scans the QR code to validate.

Why Switch to Digital?

100% Paperless

Save thousands of sheets of paper every semester. Environmentally friendly and cost-effective.

Anti-Forgery

Every pass has a SHA-256 digital signature. Impossible to fake or alter without detection.

Instant Audit

Admins can see who is outside the campus in real-time. No more flipping through logbooks.
